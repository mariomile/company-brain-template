# Activation

> A workspace is activated when it has created its first funnel and shared one dashboard with a teammate.

## What it is

The moment a new workspace has done the two things that predict it stays. Measured per workspace, not per user.

## Why it matters here

- Activated workspaces retain at 71% at 90 days; non-activated at 18% [source: [[Input/Meetings/2026-10-14 Weekly product sync]]; since: 2026-10-14]
- Time-to-first-value (TTFV) is the days between creation and activation; median 9 days today, target 3 in [[Onboarding Redesign]] [source: project note; since: 2026-09-01]

## Evidence

- Four Q3 exit interviews named "never got to a first insight" [source: [[Knowledge/Company/Customers]]; since: 2026-10-10]
