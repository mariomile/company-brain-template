# Adopt the company brain

## Context

- Decisions lived in chat threads; three times in Q3 the team rebuilt a decision it had already taken [source: [[Ada Rossi]], onboarding interview; since: 2026-10-15]
- Onboarding a new hire took two weeks of asking people [source: same]

## Options

- **A.** A Notion wiki with an AI assistant.
- **B.** A Markdown repository (this brain), operated by people and agents, with owners and review dates.
- **C.** Do nothing.

## Decision

B. One brain, this repository. Owners: [[Ada Rossi]] for Company, Customers, How We Work and the decision log; [[Ben Okafor]] for Product, Glossary and `Knowledge/Product/`. Structure changes are decisions.

## Consequences

- Gained: one source of truth that agents can read and maintain; decisions with a why.
- Lost or risked: a second place to look if people keep writing in chat; mitigated by the brain-first rule in [[How We Work]].
- Cost: the Friday review, 30 minutes per owner per week.

## Revisit when

- After 90 days, if fewer than half the meetings have minutes or fewer than 4 decisions per month are recorded.

## Dissent

None recorded.
