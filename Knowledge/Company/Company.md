# Company

> The truth about what Lumen Analytics is, for whom, and where it is going. Not the product (see [[Product]]) and not the customers in detail (see [[Customers]]).

## Now

- Lumen Analytics sells product analytics to B2B SaaS teams of 20 to 200 people that cannot afford a data team [source: [[2026-10-15 Adopt the company brain]]; since: 2026-10-15]
- Stage: seed, 11 people, founded 2024 in Bologna [source: [[Ada Rossi]], onboarding interview; since: 2026-10-15]
- Revenue model: subscription, usage-based on the Team plan since October 2026 [source: [[2026-10-01 Usage-based pricing for the Team plan]]; since: 2026-10-01]
- The one number we watch: weekly active workspaces [source: [[Active/Goals/2026-Q4]]; since: 2026-10-15]

## Mission and positioning

Analytics that a product manager can run alone. We win against spreadsheets and against tools built for data teams; we do not try to win against warehouses.

## Principles

- Ship to learn, but record what we learned: a decision without a record did not happen.
- Customers over competitors: we read [[Input/Feedback]] before we read competitor pages.
