## Rules of engagement

- Brain-first: a question about the company is answered from the brain before anyone is pinged. If the brain does not know, the answer goes into the brain.
- Decisions are recorded the day they are taken, by whoever took them, from the template.
- A meeting without minutes within 24 hours did not happen for the brain.
- Living documents are verified by their owner at the cadence in `_system/object-schema.md`; the Friday review lists who is late.
- Agents propose on living documents and act on inputs. The ladder in the Blueprint, section 6.
